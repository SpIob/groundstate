## Not shown by this source
No explicit single "average voltage" number for the 0→1 Li extraction step; the 4.0 V figure is stated as the redox potential of the Mn4+/Mn3+ couple, which spans the 4 V window.

## How it bears on the current task
Independent corroboration that the spinel reference voltage is ~4.0 V vs. Li/Li+. The benchmark-set row uses 4.0 V; this source's redox-basis framing also warns that computed equilibrium voltages for the 4 V window average over a two-plateau structure — relevant when comparing CHGNet-derived averages to literature figures.
