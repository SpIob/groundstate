## How it bears on the current task

Its energy-density ratio (701/586 ≈ 1.20) also matches the ~20% voltage advantage the screen is testing (predicted +0.26 V over LFP is smaller because the computed absolute voltages carry the D-008 offset; the relative ordering is the usable output per D-008).
