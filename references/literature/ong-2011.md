## How it bears on the current task
This is the same expression as the method-notes formula with A=Li, per-mole basis (e→F): V = −[E(x2) − E(x1) − (x2−x1)·E_Li] / (x2−x1). It originates from the Ceder group's average-voltage method (Aydinol et al., JPS 68 (1997) 664 — the PDF at ceder.berkeley.edu uses embedded bitmap fonts that defeated text extraction, so its equation was not independently re-read; lineage noted per `references/CITATION_STANDARD.md`). pymatgen 2026.9.24's `InsertionVoltagePair` (apps/battery/insertion_battery.py) implements the same energy-difference construction with per-formula-unit normalization, matching our planned `/gs-derive-voltage-capacity` implementation.
